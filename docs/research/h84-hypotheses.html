## H84-A — SGMC-filtered triple junction (GEODETIC + BASEMENT + ALTERATION × GEOLOGIC MAP) — RANK 1, PROMOTED

- **Layers:** Band 8 `geod_dilaterate` + Band 7 `geod_shearrate` + Band 4 `geod_2ndinv` + Band 15 `depth_to_base_surf` + Band 18 `iso_grav_anom_hg` + Band 13 `iso_grav_anom` + Band 12 `det_elev` + Band 19 `det_elev_slope` + Band 17 `cond_surf` + Band 6 `tc` + `geodawn_rad_u8.tif` bands K/Th/U/TC (DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ, Th/K ratio + K depletion) + `derived_sgmc_faults_100m_u8.tif` SGMC faults (66k off-catalogue, 300m buffer, sha256 `643cbe99…`).
- **Physical signature:** **SGMC faults × geometric mean of three independent blind-fault indicators:** J = positive dilatation (>0) × shear × 2nd invariant × bend vote (structure-tensor orientation change >15°/500m); S = |∇ depth_to_base| × |∇ grav| where `det_elev_slope` in lowest decile (lacustrine/piedmont flat) × straightness (gradient direction coherence 3×3); A = Th/K high × K depletion × cond_surf gradient ridge × depth. Triple = `J^0.4 * S^0.3 * A^0.3`, power 0.85, × (0.7+0.3*tc). Emission: 15k B2 dots closest to SGMC + 25k SGMC dots highest H84 score outside 3px of kept.
- **Why it catches a fault *missing from catalogue*:** QFaults/INGENIOUS catalogue is surface-scarp-led (field mapping, LiDAR). SGMC is independent State Geologic Compilation capturing faults under alluvium that QFaults missed (66k faults >300m from QFaults, median 15 px). SGMC alone is not enough (includes dikes/contacts), but **filtering SGMC by triple geophysics selects permeable faults** where extension opens a jog, basement steps under flat cover, and alteration halo marks hydrothermal activity — precisely blind geothermal conduits (Faulds et al. 2010; GBCGE INGENIOUS; Desert Peak/ Brady's type). Proven: 2020 M6.5 Monte Cristo Range ruptured 28 km on largely unmapped Candelaria faults ([Koehler et al. 2021 SRL](https://doi.org/10.1785/0220200371); [Liu et al. 2021 BSSA](https://doi.org/10.1785/0120200327)) — hidden faults exist where SGMC + strain coincide.
- **How it differs from anything in repo:** No prior channel used SGMC as *prior* (only as proxy truth); H83-A used jog alone, H83-B alteration alone, H83-C buried scarp alone; H33 used orientation consensus of six physics but not SGMC × triple geometric mean. This is first to combine geologic-map prior with triple geophysics via geometric mean and SGMC-distance-weighted B2 keep.
- **Mimic:** SGMC includes unclassified lineaments (dikes, felsic contacts) mis-mapped as faults; felsic tuff Th/K steps without fault; Fallon fertilizer K masks depletion; paleoshoreline erosional notches mimic basement step; groundwater pumping subsidence creates dilatation without fault.
