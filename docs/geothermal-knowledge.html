Why blind faults host geothermal vents

  • Great Basin extensional tectonics: Basin and Range extension creates normal faults with dilational jogs at bends/stepovers where permeability is highest; these jogs host blind geothermal systems (Faulds et al., GBCGE INGENIOUS framework — gbcge.org/ingenious).
  • Lahontan cover: 60% of NW Nevada is Quaternary alluvium/lacustrine (Lake Lahontan ~12.7 ka highstand). Faults offsetting basement under 100 m of lake clays have no topo scarp, so USGS Quaternary mapping misses them; but depth_to_base_surf step and isostatic gravity gradient remain (GeoDAWN).
  • 2020 Monte Cristo case: M6.5 earthquake ruptured 28 km on largely unmapped Candelaria fault strands, proving hidden faults exist where strain is high but scarps absent — SRL & BSSA.
  • Alteration signature: Hydrothermal upwelling bleaches K, elevates Th/K along fault; detectable in GeoDAWN radiometric K/Th/U grids (DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ) and corroborated by GDR 1391 well temps >30°C (GDR 1391, CC BY 4.0).
  • Metric budget: Organizer DTI α0.2 β0.8 R300m means each emitted dot costs 0.2 denominator; only dot whose kernel weight k > α·DTI (≈0.054 at 0.27) improves score — so mass must be spent where hidden truth is densest (dilational jogs), not on catalogue flank (budget argument in problem page & Tversky index).
