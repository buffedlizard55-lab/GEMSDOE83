Geothermal fault research notes

These are research questions, not verified findings. The relevant external pages and literature were not fetched in this session. The competition task is fault-oriented; a fault prediction does not establish a geothermal vent, reservoir, temperature, permeability, or resource.

Questions to test against official sources

Concealed fault geometry

Can magnetic/gravity structure or basement-depth boundaries reveal buried faults where surface mapping is weak? Check the official feature definitions and their spatial resolution before interpreting a lineament.

USGS GeoDAWN source · not fetched in this session.

Faults versus geothermal systems

Which fault geometries coincide with actual geothermal evidence, and which are ordinary tectonic or lithologic structures? Anomaly co-location alone cannot answer this.

GBCGE INGENIOUS and GDR 1391 are supplied review links, not sources verified here.

Alternative explanations

Intrusive contacts, sedimentary boundaries, weathering, survey seams, induced seismicity, hydrologic processes, and human land use can mimic fault-like patterns. Every proposed detector must name and test its non-fault mimic.

Candidate-specific mimics are in the hypothesis table.

Research integrity rules

  • Use official source metadata and preserve product/version/hashes.
  • Separate source facts, owner-mirror claims, hypotheses, holdout measurements, and organizer receipts.
  • Do not infer vent location or geothermal favorability from the competition fault labels alone.
  • Keep a negative result and its confounders; do not rewrite it as success.
